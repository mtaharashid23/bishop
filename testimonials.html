He moved up from Cosby to be an outstanding principal at Lee- Davis and Patriot High Schools. His book 'What They Don't Teach You in Principal's School', is well written and is an excellent book for educators training to become administrators or those who are already in administration. Highly recommended, enjoy the book."

Ted Salmon
Chesterfield Co Public Schools, Retired
